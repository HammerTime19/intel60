About

Why Intel60 exists

Security teams don't lack news. They lack time. Intel60 reads the security press every 30 minutes and turns each story into a 60-word brief: what happened, who's affected and what defenders should do, rated by severity and backed by vulnerability data.

One card per story, even when five outlets cover it. Read the day's news in about five minutes, then get back to work.

Where the news comes from

BleepingComputerThe Hacker NewsSecurityWeekKrebs on SecurityDark ReadingThe RecordCyberScoopSANS ISCCISA KEV catalog

Vulnerability scores come from NVD (CVSS) and FIRST (EPSS). Every card links to the original article.

How severity is rated

  • Critical: exploitation in the wild or a CISA KEV addition in widely used products; unauthenticated remote code execution or CVSS 9.0+ in enterprise software and edge devices; breaches or ransomware with major real-world impact.
  • High: serious vulnerabilities without known exploitation, notable breaches, and major new malware or threat-actor campaigns.
  • Info: research, analysis, arrests and sentencing, policy and regulation, and industry news.

How it works

  1. Every 30 minutes, an automated run checks 8 security outlets and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
  2. It skips stories it has already seen, anything older than 48 hours, and ads or webinars.
  3. Claude (Anthropic's AI model) writes each headline and 60-word summary, rates severity, assigns a category and merges duplicate coverage.
  4. Each CVE gets its CVSS score, its exploit probability and its KEV status.
  5. The cards are published here, and critical stories are sent to Telegram.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies and no ads. Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter. Your watchlist and settings are saved only in your own browser and never sent anywhere.

Questions

Is Intel60 free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no paywall and no ads.

How often is it updated?

Every 30 minutes, around the clock. The "Live" marker at the top shows when the last update ran.

Can I trust AI-written summaries?

They're a fast way to triage, not a replacement for the source. Every card links to the original reporting, and CVE scores come straight from NVD, FIRST and CISA, not from the AI. Check the original before acting.

How do I get alerts?

Join the Telegram channel at t.me/intel60news. Critical stories arrive as they break, plus a morning brief at 7 AM Eastern.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open intel60.app on your phone for the swipeable card app, then choose Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Install app (Android).
